How to play Kashi Blocks

Kashi Blocks is a free block puzzle game. Kashi is the Persian word for the glazed tiles that cover the mosques and palaces of Isfahan, and every block in the game is one of those tiles.

Rules and controls

  1. You get three pieces at a time. Drag a piece from the bottom onto the 8 by 8 board.
  2. Fill a whole row or a whole column to clear it. You can clear rows and columns at the same time.
  3. When all three pieces are placed, you get three new ones.
  4. The game ends when none of your pieces fits on the board.

Scoring

  • Every tile you place scores 1 point.
  • Clearing lines scores much more, and clearing several lines at once scores even more.
  • Clear lines on several moves in a row for a combo bonus, up to four times the points.
  • Empty the whole board for a perfect clear bonus of 300 points.

Tips

  • Keep space free for the big 3 by 3 square and the long 5-tile lines.
  • Do not fill the middle first. Build rows and columns along the edges.
  • Pieces that do not fit anywhere are shown faded, so plan your next move.

Kashi Blocks is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed, and your game is saved on your device.
