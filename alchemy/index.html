How to play Afsoon Alchemy

Afsoon Alchemy is a free merge game. You are a royal alchemist: brew sparks, merge them into potions and crystals, and fill the orders of the royal court.

Rules and controls

  1. Tap the brew button to make a new item. Each brew costs one energy, and energy refills over time.
  2. Drag two identical items onto each other to merge them into a better item.
  3. Items with a green dot are wanted by the court. Make what the court asks for, then tap the order to deliver it.
  4. If the board gets full, merge or sell items to make room.

Tips

  • Keep a few free spaces on the board so you can always brew.
  • Tap any item to see what it is and what it turns into.

Afsoon Alchemy is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed.
