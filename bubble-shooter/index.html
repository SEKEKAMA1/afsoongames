How to play Afsoon Bubble Shooter

Afsoon Bubble Shooter is a free bubble shooter game with jewel bubbles in turquoise, lapis, gold and ruby. Aim, bounce and match colours to clear the board.

Rules and controls

  1. Shoot a bubble into the board. When three or more bubbles of the same colour touch, they pop.
  2. Bubbles that are no longer connected to the top fall down and give bonus points.
  3. Every few shots without a pop, a new row of bubbles comes down. If the bubbles reach the line at the bottom, the game ends.
  4. Clear the whole board to get a bonus and move to the next level.
  5. On a phone: drag to aim and let go to shoot. Tap the small bubble to swap it. On a computer: aim with the mouse and click, or press Space to swap.

Tips

  • Aim for bubbles that hold up big groups. Dropping them scores much more than popping.
  • Use the walls: a bank shot can reach places a straight shot cannot.
  • Swap when the bubble in your shooter doesn't fit anywhere good.

Afsoon Bubble Shooter is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed.
