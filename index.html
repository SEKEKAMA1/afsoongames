About us

A name that means magic

In Persian, afsoon means a spell or an enchantment — the magic that captivates you and won't let go. That is exactly what we want every one of our games to do.

Afsoon Games is a young independent studio. We draw inspiration from the myths, art and mysteries of ancient Persia, and turn them into games for players everywhere, on mobile and in the browser.
