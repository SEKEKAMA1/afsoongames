How to play Afsoon Water Sort

Afsoon Water Sort is a free colour sort puzzle. Glass bottles are filled with layers of rosewater, saffron, pomegranate, turquoise and other colours. Pour them from bottle to bottle until each bottle holds a single colour.

Rules and controls

  1. Tap a bottle to pick it up, then tap another bottle to pour into it.
  2. You can only pour onto the same colour, or into an empty bottle. A bottle holds four layers.
  3. All layers of the same colour on top are poured together, as far as there is room.
  4. The level is done when every bottle is full of one colour or empty.
  5. Every level has 5 undos and one extra empty bottle you can add when you are stuck. Restart gives them back.
  6. On a computer you can also press 1 to 9 to pick bottles, Z to undo and R to restart.

Tips

  • Keep one bottle empty as long as you can. It is your most useful tool.
  • Start with the colours that are already near the top of several bottles.
  • Finish one colour at a time, then the bottles free up for the rest.
  • Finish a level in few moves and without the extra bottle to earn three stars.

Afsoon Water Sort is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed, and your progress is saved on your device. Every level can be solved.
