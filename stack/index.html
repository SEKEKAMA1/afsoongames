How to play Afsoon Stack

Afsoon Stack is a free stockpile card game in the family of Spite & Malice. Build the shared piles in the middle from 1 up to 12 and be the first player to get rid of every card in your stockpile. Play against one to five computer players on your phone or computer.

Rules

  1. Every player gets a stockpile (15, 20 or 30 cards) with only the top card face up, and five cards in hand.
  2. Up to four piles are built in the middle. Each starts with a 1 and goes up in order to 12. A full pile is cleared and its cards go back into the deck.
  3. On your turn you can play the top card of your stockpile, cards from your hand and the top cards of your discard piles.
  4. The gold Afsoon card is wild and counts as any number.
  5. If you play every card in your hand, draw five more and keep going.
  6. End your turn by putting one card from your hand on one of your four discard piles.
  7. The first player with an empty stockpile wins.

Tips

  • Your stockpile card is the most important card: build the middle piles towards it.
  • Keep your wild cards for the moment they let you play your stock card.
  • Put the same numbers together on a discard pile, or stack them high to low, so they come back in a useful order.
  • Watch the other players' stock cards and try not to build a pile right up to the number they need.
