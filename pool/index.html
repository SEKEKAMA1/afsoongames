How to play Afsoon 8-Ball Pool

Afsoon 8-Ball Pool is a free billiards game on a Persian table with a walnut frame and turquoise cloth. The balls roll, bounce off the cushions and drop into the pockets like on a real table. Play the computer on three levels, or a friend on the same phone or computer.

Rules

  1. One player breaks the rack. The white ball can be placed anywhere behind the line.
  2. The first player to pot a ball after the break gets that group: solids (1–7) or stripes (9–15). The other player gets the other group.
  3. Pot one of your own balls and you shoot again. Miss, and it is the other player's turn.
  4. It is a foul if the white ball goes in, if you hit no ball, or if the first ball you hit is not one of yours. After a foul the other player gets the white ball in hand and can put it anywhere.
  5. When all your balls are gone, pot the black 8-ball to win. Pot the 8-ball too early, or with a foul, and you lose.

Tips

  • The dotted line shows where the white ball goes; the gold line shows where the ball you hit will roll.
  • Use the small arrow buttons to turn the aim a tiny bit for exact shots.
  • Tap high on the spin ball for follow (the white ball rolls on), low for draw (it comes back).
  • On a computer: arrow keys turn the aim, hold Shift to turn faster.
