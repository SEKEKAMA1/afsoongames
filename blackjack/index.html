How to play Afsoon Blackjack

Afsoon Blackjack is a free blackjack (21) card game with real casino rules. Play against the dealer with free play chips – no money, no download and no account needed.

Rules

  1. Place your bet with the chips (minimum 5), then tap Deal. You and the dealer get two cards; one dealer card stays face down.
  2. Number cards count their number, J, Q and K count 10, and an Ace counts 1 or 11.
  3. Hit to take another card, Stand to keep your hand. Go over 21 and you bust and lose the bet.
  4. Double: double your bet on your first two cards and take exactly one more card.
  5. Split: two cards of the same value can be split into two hands (up to four hands). Split Aces get one card each.
  6. Surrender: on your first two cards you can give up and get half your bet back.
  7. When the dealer shows an Ace you can take insurance, which pays 2 to 1 if the dealer has blackjack.
  8. The dealer turns over the hidden card and must hit until 17 or more, and stands on all 17s.
  9. Beat the dealer and you win 1 to 1. An Ace with a 10-card on your first two cards is Blackjack and pays 3 to 2. Same total is a push and your bet comes back.

The game is dealt from a 6-deck shoe that is shuffled again when it gets low. If you run out of chips, the house gives you 1,000 new ones.

Tips

  • Tap Hint any time to see the best play by basic strategy.
  • Always split Aces and 8s. Never split 10s or 5s.
  • Double on 11, and on 10 when the dealer does not show a 10 or Ace.
  • Stand on 12–16 when the dealer shows 2 to 6 – the dealer is likely to bust.
  • On a computer: H = hit, S = stand, D = double, P = split, R = surrender, Space = deal.
