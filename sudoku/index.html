A game by Afsoon Games. Fill every row, column and 3×3 box with the numbers 1 to 9.

How to play Afsoon Sudoku

Afsoon Sudoku has fresh, free sudoku puzzles on four levels from Easy to Expert. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Rules and controls

  1. Fill the grid so that every row, every column and every 3 by 3 box contains the numbers 1 to 9 once.
  2. Tap a cell, then a number. Use notes to write small candidate numbers in a cell.
  3. On a computer: arrow keys to move, 1 to 9 to fill in, Backspace to clear and N for notes.
  4. Use a hint if you get stuck.

Tips

  • Look for rows, columns or boxes that are almost full.
  • If a number can only go in one cell of a box, it must go there.

Afsoon Sudoku is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed.
