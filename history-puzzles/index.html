How to play History Building Puzzles

History Building Puzzles is a free match-3 game where you rebuild ten famous buildings of the world, from Persepolis and the Pyramids to the Eiffel Tower.

Rules and controls

  1. Swap two neighbouring tiles to line up three or more of the same kind.
  2. Each match collects building materials for the level goal.
  3. Every level rebuilds part of a wonder. Finish all five levels of a building to restore it and unlock the next one.
  4. Finish with moves to spare to earn more stars.

Tips

  • Matches of four or five tiles make special tiles that clear much more at once.
  • Work near the bottom of the board: falling tiles often make free extra matches.

History Building Puzzles is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed.
