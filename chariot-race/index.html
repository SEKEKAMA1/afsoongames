How to play Chariot Race

Chariot Race is a free racing game set in ancient Persia. Drive a royal chariot past the columns of Persepolis and race against five rivals.

Rules and controls

  1. Steer left and right with the arrow keys or A and D, or with the buttons on a phone.
  2. Dodge rocks and jars on the road.
  3. Grab gold darics to fill your royal boost, then use it to race ahead.
  4. Finish in the top three to unlock the next road. Your progress is saved on your device.

Tips

  • Save your boost for the last part of the race.
  • Hitting obstacles slows you down more than a slightly longer line around them.

Chariot Race is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed.
