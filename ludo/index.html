How to play Afsoon Ludo (Mench)

Afsoon Ludo is the family dice game known in Iran as Mench. Race your four pieces once around the board and into your own home row. Play against the computer, or with two to four friends passing one phone or computer around.

Rules

  1. Every player has four pieces that start in their corner.
  2. Roll a 6 to bring a piece out onto your coloured start square. Every 6 gives you another roll.
  3. If none of your pieces is out on the track, you get three tries to roll a 6.
  4. Move a piece forward by the number you rolled. You cannot land on your own piece.
  5. Land exactly on a rival's piece and it goes back to its corner.
  6. After one full lap a piece turns into your home row. You need the exact number to move inside it, and you cannot jump over your own pieces there.
  7. The first player with all four pieces in the home row wins.

Tips

  • Tap the dice to roll. When more than one piece can move, the pieces that can move glow – tap one. Dashed rings show where they will land.
  • Keep your pieces more than six squares ahead of a rival when you can.
  • Bring new pieces out early – more pieces on the track means more choices.
  • On a computer: Space rolls the dice, and keys 1–4 pick a move.
