How to play Shatranj: Persian Chess

Shatranj is free online chess with a Persian look. Chess grew up in ancient Persia, and the word checkmate comes from the Persian shah mat: the king is helpless.

Rules and controls

  1. Play against the computer on three levels, or against a friend on the same device.
  2. Tap a piece, then tap one of the dots to move it there.
  3. All standard chess rules are included: castling, en passant and promotion.
  4. Checkmate the other king to win. Stalemate, repeated positions and the fifty-move rule end the game in a draw.

Tips

  • Control the centre of the board early.
  • Bring out your knights and bishops before moving the queen.

Shatranj: Persian Chess is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed.
