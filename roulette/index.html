How to play Afsoon Roulette

Afsoon Roulette is a free European roulette game with a single zero. Put your chips on the table, spin the wheel and watch the ball roll, drop and bounce into a pocket. You play with free play chips – no money, no download and no account needed.

Rules

  1. Pick a chip (1, 5, 25, 100 or 500) and tap the table to place it. Tap again to add more.
  2. Tap Spin. The ball lands in one of 37 pockets: 0 to 36. Zero is green; the other numbers are red or black.
  3. Every bet that covers the winning number is paid; all other chips are taken by the house.
  4. Undo takes back your last chip, Clear takes all chips back, Double doubles every bet and Re-bet places the bets of the last round again.

Bets and payouts

  • Straight up – one number: pays 35 to 1
  • Split – two numbers next to each other (tap the line between them): 17 to 1
  • Street – a row of three numbers (tap the bottom edge of the row): 11 to 1
  • Corner – four numbers (tap the point where they meet): 8 to 1
  • First four – 0, 1, 2 and 3: 8 to 1
  • Six line – two rows, six numbers: 5 to 1
  • Column (2 to 1) or dozen (1st, 2nd, 3rd 12): 2 to 1
  • Red or black, even or odd, 1 to 18 or 19 to 36: 1 to 1

European roulette has only one zero, so the house edge is 2.7% – lower than American roulette with its extra double zero. If you run out of chips, the house gives you 1,000 new ones.

Tips

  • Even-money bets (red, black, odd, even, low, high) win almost half the time; a straight-up number wins rarely but pays 35 times your chip.
  • Every spin is random: past numbers do not change the next one.
  • Tap the dealer button at the top to hear the dealer: she calls the winning number, how much you won or lost that round, and your total for this session.
  • On a computer: Space = spin, right-click a bet to remove it.
