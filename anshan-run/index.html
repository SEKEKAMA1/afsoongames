How to play Anshan Run

Anshan Run is a free endless runner set in ancient Anshan, the home city of the first Achaemenid kings. You play a royal messenger carrying the king's clay tablet across the mud-brick rooftops.

Rules and controls

  1. Jump with a tap on the screen, or with Space, W or the Up arrow on a keyboard.
  2. Jump over the gaps between rooftops and dodge the jars in your way.
  3. Collect gold coins as you run. The farther you get, the higher your score.
  4. Press P or Escape to pause.

Tips

  • Jump a little before the edge of a roof, not at the last moment.
  • Watch ahead: the run gets faster the longer you survive.

Anshan Run is free and runs in your browser on phone, tablet and computer. No download or account needed.
